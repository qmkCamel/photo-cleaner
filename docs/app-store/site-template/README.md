# TrueKeep App Store Static Page Template

This folder is a ready-to-host static template for the App Store Support URL and Privacy Policy URL.

## Files

- `index.html`: landing page linking to support and privacy.
- `support.html`: support FAQ and App Review note.
- `privacy.html`: privacy policy.
- `styles.css`: shared responsive styling.

## Before Hosting

- Replace the support-contact placeholder with a monitored email address or support form URL.
- Confirm the final public domain, such as `https://<domain>/truekeep/support.html` and `https://<domain>/truekeep/privacy.html`.
- Recheck that the Privacy Policy still matches the shipped build before submission.
- Verify the hosted URLs are accessible without login, geoblocking, or redirects that App Review cannot follow.

## Current Status

The pages are deployable static HTML, but they are not final App Store URLs until the support contact and hosting location are confirmed.
