隐私政策

照片与视频在本机处理。以下说明 App、本网站和主动支持联系各自的数据处理方式。

中文

1. 适用范围与联系

本政策适用于 TrueKeep / 留真 iOS App 及其支持网站。隐私问题或相关请求请联系 qmkhaust@gmail.com。

2. 照片权限与本地分析

App 会在请求系统照片权限前说明用途。获准后，通过 Apple Photos 框架读取可访问的照片和视频元数据、缩略图，以及精确重复校验需要的本地资源数据。Vision 图像分析和资源摘要比较均在设备上完成；App 不会将这些内容上传给开发者或第三方。

有限照片访问是有效状态，你可以只允许部分项目，并随时在 iOS 设置中调整或撤销权限。扫描不会主动下载仅存于 iCloud 的资源；无法在本地取得的资源可能不参与完整分析。

3. 本机保存的信息

App 在本地保存是否完成引导的设置，以及你主动确认保留的 Photos 资源 ID，用于在重新启动和扫描后记住选择。它不因此保存照片或视频副本。读取的缩略图用于本机显示和内存缓存。

你可以在“已确认保留”中取消或重置保留记录。卸载并删除 App 数据也会删除其本地设置；卸载而保留数据、系统备份或恢复的行为由 iOS 管理，可能保留或恢复设置。照片图库的原始项目由系统 Photos 管理。

4. 上传、跟踪与数据共享

当前 App 不需要账号，没有照片上传、广告、分析、第三方崩溃报告或跨 App 跟踪 SDK。开发者不会通过 App 收集或出售你的照片、视频及本地保留记录。扫描结果只是复核候选，不会自动触发删除。

5. 删除与恢复

你可以先将候选加入复核箱，或在复核组中直接发起删除。无论哪条路径，都需要先选择项目，再明确进行第二次确认，App 才会请求系统删除。复核箱内的项目尚未被删除。

启用 iCloud 照片时，删除可能同步至 iCloud 和其他设备。可恢复项目通常位于系统“照片”的“最近删除”中，具体期限和可恢复性以系统显示为准；永久删除或超过期限后，App 无法恢复。

6. 本网站与支持邮件

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，没有加入分析脚本、广告或追踪像素。访问网站时，GitHub 可能处理 IP 地址、请求时间等服务访问数据，详见 GitHub 隐私声明。这与 App 的本地照片处理相独立。

如果你主动发送支持邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址及自愿提供的内容，并用于回复和处理请求。请不要发送照片原件或不必要的敏感信息。邮件服务提供商也会处理邮件传输和存储；支持往来仅在处理问题及必要后续事项期间保留。你可以通过支持邮箱提出访问、更正或删除请求，我们会在适用法律允许和要求的范围内处理。

7. 政策更新

如果 App 的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策及相应的 App Store 隐私说明。页面标注最新生效日期。

English

1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the TrueKeep / 留真 iOS app and its support website. Contact qmkhaust@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

2. Photos access and local analysis

The app explains Photos access before requesting system permission. With permission, it reads accessible photo and video metadata, thumbnails, and local resource data needed to check exact duplicates through Apple's Photos framework. Vision analysis and resource hashing run on your device. The app does not upload this content to the developer or third parties.

Limited Photos Access is supported. You can adjust or revoke access in iOS Settings. Scanning does not download iCloud-only resources; unavailable local resources may be excluded from complete analysis.

3. Information stored on your device

The app stores onboarding completion and the Photos resource identifiers you explicitly mark as confirmed keeps. These records preserve your choices across launches and scans without creating copies of your photos or videos. Thumbnails are used for local display and memory caching.

You can remove or reset confirmed-keep records in Settings. Deleting the app and its data removes local settings. Offloading, system backups, and restoration are managed by iOS and may preserve or restore settings. Original library items remain under the control of the system Photos library.

4. Uploads, tracking, and sharing

The current app requires no account and includes no photo upload, advertising, analytics, third-party crash-reporting, or cross-app tracking SDK. The developer does not collect or sell your photos, videos, or local keep records through the app. Scan results are review candidates and do not automatically trigger deletion.

5. Deletion and recovery

You may add candidates to Review Bin or initiate deletion directly from a review group. Both paths require selecting items and a second explicit confirmation before the app requests system deletion. Items in Review Bin have not been deleted.

With iCloud Photos enabled, deletion may sync to iCloud and other devices. Check Recently Deleted in Apple's Photos app for recovery availability and deadlines. TrueKeep cannot recover items that have been permanently deleted or passed the system recovery window.

6. Website and support email

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages without added analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking pixels. GitHub may process service access information such as IP addresses and request times under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This is separate from local photo processing in the app.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use it to handle and reply to your request. Do not send original photos or unnecessary sensitive information. Email providers also process message transmission and storage. Support correspondence is retained for handling the issue and necessary follow-up. You may request access, correction, or deletion through the support address, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. Policy changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the relevant App Store privacy disclosures will be updated. The effective date is shown on this page.
