# TrueKeep App Store Compliance Answers Draft

Updated: 2026-06-11

This draft prepares the non-metadata App Store Connect answers that still need to be entered by the Apple Developer account owner. Final values must be reviewed in App Store Connect before submission.

## Official References

- Age rating is a required app information field and is determined by the App Store Connect questionnaire.
  Source: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions/
- App Store Connect asks export-compliance questions to determine whether encryption documentation is required.
  Source: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/overview-of-export-compliance/
- Apple says apps using encryption limited to the Apple operating system do not need encryption documentation in App Store Connect.
  Source: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/export-compliance-documentation-for-encryption/

## Age Rating Draft

Expected positioning: likely suitable for the lowest age rating generated by App Store Connect, because the app is a private photo-library utility with no social, commerce, web, or entertainment content. App Store Connect must calculate the final rating.

Draft questionnaire posture:

- Cartoon or fantasy violence: none.
- Realistic violence: none.
- Prolonged graphic or sadistic realistic violence: none.
- Profanity or crude humor: none.
- Mature or suggestive themes: none.
- Horror or fear themes: none.
- Medical or treatment information: none.
- Alcohol, tobacco, drug use, or references: none.
- Simulated gambling: none.
- Contests: no.
- Loot boxes: no.
- Unrestricted web access: no.
- Gambling: no.
- User-generated content publication or social feed: no.
- Messaging or chat between users: no.
- Location sharing: no.

Important nuance:

- The app can access the user's own Photos library after permission. It does not publish, share, or moderate user-generated content.
- Screenshots used for App Store assets must not show real private user photos unless those photos are owned and cleared for marketing use.

## Export Compliance Draft

Draft answer:

- The current build does not implement custom cryptography.
- The current build does not use networking APIs for app features.
- The current build does not include account login, web views, subscriptions, payments, analytics, ads, or server sync.
- If App Store Connect asks whether encryption is limited to encryption provided by the Apple operating system, current code evidence supports that no additional encryption documentation should be required.

Recheck before submission if any of these are added:

- Networking or server upload.
- Account login.
- Subscription purchase or receipt validation.
- Support chat.
- Crash reporting or analytics SDK.
- Any custom cryptographic library.

## Content Rights Draft

- App icon and logo assets are project-owned/generated for TrueKeep.
- App Store screenshot candidates are captured from the app UI.
- Sample thumbnail tiles in screenshots are generated UI placeholders, not third-party photos.
- No third-party music, video, stock photo, celebrity image, or external brand asset is included in the current build.

## Category Draft

Primary category draft:

- Photo & Video

Secondary category draft:

- Utilities

Reason:

- The core user job is reviewing and cleaning the local Photos library.
- The utility framing is secondary and should not turn the product into a generic phone-cleaner claim.

## Submission Notes To Keep Accurate

- Do not claim system cache cleaning, virus removal, phone speedup, or automatic cleanup.
- Do not imply photos are uploaded or analyzed in the cloud.
- Do not imply heuristic similar/blurry/accidental classifications are certain.
- State that deletion requires user review and confirmation.
- State that iCloud Photos deletion may sync to other devices.
